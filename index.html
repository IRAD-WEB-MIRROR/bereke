Bereke: Irad's largest supermarket chain

Bereke means plenty. We run 144 stores in Irad's three cities: 76 in Talas, 53 in Nurzhan and 15 in Iyık Janyl. Twenty are full Bereke supermarkets on the main roads; the other 124 are Bereke Xpress shops in the ground floors of the blocks, a few minutes' walk from home.

Bereke supermarkets

The big stores, 800 to 2,500 square metres, carry the full range: fresh produce, meat and fish counters, a bakery, dairy, household goods and the Savma shelf on fast days. Talas Süt, Talas Nan Kombinatı, Altyn-Bulak and Shu Dala are on our shelves next to Kazakh, Russian, Uzbek and Turkish brands.

Bereke Xpress

Since the 2016–17 refit the neighbourhood shops trade as Bereke Xpress: open fridges, card terminals and longer hours, 7:00 to 23:00. Fourteen are open round the clock, by bus stations, hospitals and the main crossroads. In every Xpress you can top up your phone and buy an IradLoto ticket.

Paying

Prices are in som. Every till takes cash and Talasbank cards.
