Bereke/Code of conduct

Code of conduct

How we work with our customers, our staff and our suppliers.

Our customers

The price on the shelf is the price at the till. If they differ, you pay the lower one. We sell no alcohol or tobacco to anyone under 18, and no alcohol from 23:00 to 8:00.

Our staff

Staff are paid on time, twice a month, and work no more than the hours in their contract. Overtime is paid. Every member of staff can raise a problem with their manager or, in confidence, on the staff line.

Our suppliers

We choose suppliers on quality, price and reliability. Our buyers may not accept gifts, payments, meals or favours. Suppliers are paid on the dates in their contracts.

Food safety

Goods past their date come off the shelf the same day. Fresh meat, fish and milk are kept and checked at the temperatures set by the sanitary rules.

Report a breach

Write to ethics@berekemarket.com. You do not have to give your name.
